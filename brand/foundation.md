# Foundation

The core of what TEN:TEN is, why it exists, and who it is for. Everything else in the brand kit extends from this file. Read this first.

---

## The Brand

**TEN:TEN** is an experimental watchmaking brand that rescues and restores orphaned mechanical movements, then builds entirely new watches around them. Each movement becomes the starting point for a unique design, preserving both the machine and its history while giving it new life.

The name references the classic hand position used in watch photography — keeping logos, complications, and details visible while creating the balanced upward "smile" of the hands. It rewards those who know watches without requiring that knowledge to work. Simple, memorable, and designed to sit naturally on a dial.

---

## The Provocation

We live in a world of increasingly disposable products, yet we still crave things that are beautifully made and built to last.

Countless orphaned watch movements have outlived the watches that once gave them purpose.

What if we designed new mechanical watches without designing new mechanical movements — using the ingenuity of the past as the starting point for something new?

---

## The Market Gap

As products become harder to repair and easier to replace, our relationship with the things we own is becoming increasingly temporary. Yet there remains a desire for objects that are well-crafted, enduring, and worth caring for.

---

## Core Mission

To carry the ingenuity of the past forward through restoration, reuse, and new design.

---

## Audience

TEN:TEN has three distinct audiences, each arriving through a different door.

### The Horology Native — Comes for the movement.

Already fascinated by mechanical watches, movements, complications, restoration, and watchmaking history. They understand why these orphaned mechanisms are worth saving and appreciate seeing them interpreted in new ways.

**Motivation:** "I know what that is, and I've never seen anyone treat it like this."

### The Design Obsessive — Comes for the interpretation.

Interested in industrial design, architecture, fashion, furniture, art, materials, and beautifully considered objects. Drawn to the idea that the movement itself becomes the creative brief.

**Motivation:** "I've never seen a watch designed this way before."

### The Reusable Consumer — Comes for the second life.

Tired of products designed to be replaced rather than repaired. They value objects that endure, accumulate history, and can be restored or reimagined instead of discarded.

**Motivation:** "I love that something forgotten was made important again."

### Audience Framework

- **Horology** — What is worth saving?
- **Design** — What could it become?
- **Reuse** — Why should it have another life?

## Brand Principles

These are the beliefs that underpin every decision TEN:TEN makes — about products, about design, about communication.

- Some technologies deserve another life.
- Every movement generates its own watch.
- Its history becomes part of the product.
- We don't manufacture scarcity. We inherit it.
- We don't dismantle history to manufacture products.
- The studio works with what would otherwise disappear.
- Preservation through continued use.
- The future doesn't always have to start from scratch.

## The One-Line Brand Truth

**The movement may be vintage. The brand isn't.**
