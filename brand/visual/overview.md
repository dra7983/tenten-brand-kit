# Visual Identity

TEN:TEN's visual identity is built on restraint in the brand layer and color in the product layer. The brand infrastructure — type, layout, UI — is monochrome. Color arrives through photography and the watches themselves. This discipline means every watch that appears commands attention, and every photograph earns its place.

See the individual files in this folder for full detail:

- `typography.md` — typefaces, weights, casing, hierarchy
- `color.md` — palette, accent usage, photography as color system
- `photography.md` — shoot style, categories, mood
- `logo.md` — logotype configurations, usage rules, clear space

---

## Core Visual Principles

**The brand is black and white. The product is color.**
No brand-level color decisions compete with the watches. Color is reserved for photography and product design.

**Editorial density over empty luxury.**
Layouts are content-forward with considered spacing — closer to a well-designed magazine than a high-end e-commerce template. Things are allowed to be close together if they are close for a reason.

**Swiss discipline, not Swiss cliché.**
Helvetica Neue earns its place here. The Swiss connection is real — Swiss typeface, Swiss watchmaking heritage — but TEN:TEN is not performing Swiss minimalism. It uses the tools of that tradition to do something contemporary with them.

**The movement is always the subject.**
In photography, in copy, in product naming — the movement comes first. The watch exists because the movement did.
