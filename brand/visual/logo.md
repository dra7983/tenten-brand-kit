# Logo

---

## The Mark

The TEN:TEN logotype is set in Helvetica Neue. TEN:TEN is the only place in the brand system where all-caps appears — it is a fixed mark, not a style to be replicated elsewhere.

The colon is structural. It sits at cap height rather than baseline, functioning as a dividing element between two equal halves rather than as punctuation. It references a ratio, a timestamp, and the relationship between past movement and new watch. In the classic watch hand position it is also, quietly, the hands themselves.

---

## Approved Configurations

### Full Lockup

TEN:TEN set in Helvetica Neue Bold, stacked above WATCHES set in Helvetica Neue Light with generous letter-spacing.

**Use for:** Packaging, signage, brand contexts, marketing materials, anywhere the brand needs its full identity present.

### Wordmark Only

TEN:TEN alone, without the WATCHES descriptor.

**Use for:** Watch dials, compact applications, contexts where the descriptor would be too small to read legibly, secondary appearances within a layout that already carries the full lockup.

---

## Usage Rules

**Always use approved files.** Never recreate the logotype by typing TEN:TEN in Helvetica Neue. The approved mark has specific spacing and proportions. Use the files from the media library.

**Colorways:**
- Black logotype on white or light backgrounds
- White logotype on black or dark backgrounds
- No other color versions

**Clear space:** Maintain clear space around the logotype equal to the height of the "T" on all sides. Nothing enters this space — no other type, no imagery, no graphic elements.

**Minimum size:** The full lockup should never appear smaller than 80px wide in digital contexts, or 20mm wide in print. The wordmark alone should never appear smaller than 60px / 15mm.

**What not to do:**
- Do not stretch or distort the mark
- Do not add a drop shadow or any effect
- Do not place it on a colored background (only black or white)
- Do not outline or reverse the colon independently
- Do not use the logotype as a pattern or texture element
- Do not reproduce the logotype in the accent color (#BE3F00) — it is a black and white mark

---

## The Colon as System Element

Outside of the logotype, the colon appears as a structural device in TEN:TEN layouts — as a dividing element in diptych compositions, as a separator in product naming conventions, and as a recurring typographic motif. These uses reference the logotype but are distinct from it. They are set in Helvetica Neue at whatever weight is appropriate to the context, not in the fixed proportions of the mark.
