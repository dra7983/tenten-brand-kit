# Color

---

## The System

TEN:TEN's color system has one rule that governs everything else:

**The brand is black and white. The product is color.**

Brand infrastructure — type, layouts, UI, borders, backgrounds — is monochrome. Color arrives exclusively through photography and the watches themselves. This means every product image commands attention the moment it appears. Color is earned, not ambient.

---

## Primary Colors

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Black | `#000000` | Type, borders, backgrounds, logotype |
| Primary White | `#FFFFFF` | Backgrounds, reversed type, product photography ground |

---

## Accent Color

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Burnt Orange | `#BE3F00` | Interactive states only — hover, active links, focus indicators, cursor elements |

The accent color is a deep, oxidised orange. It references the patina of aged brass and worn metal without being literal about it. It exists exclusively in the interface layer.

**Accent usage rules:**
- Hover and active states on links and buttons
- Focus indicators for accessibility
- Active navigation states
- Never on photography
- Never on product imagery
- Never as a background color
- Never in print or physical applications
- Never used decoratively

---

## Utility Colors

These are not brand colors. They are infrastructure.

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Light Grey | `#F5F5F5` | Page backgrounds behind white cards, subtle section breaks |
| Mid Grey | `#AAAAAA` | Captions, metadata, hairline dividers, secondary UI text |

---

## Color in Photography

Photography is TEN:TEN's primary color system. All color decisions in campaign and editorial work happen through the lens, not through the brand palette.

**What this means in practice:**
- Workshop and lifestyle photography: full color
- Product photography: full color watch on white or black ground
- Campaign photography: see `photography.md` for the selective color treatment established by the hero asset

The watches are always the most colorful thing in any TEN:TEN image. Nothing else competes.

---

## What TEN:TEN Color Is Not

- It is not a heritage palette of warm creams and aged tones
- It is not a luxury palette of gold and navy
- It is not a tech palette of cool blues and gradients
- It is not a sustainability palette of earth greens

The brand is black and white. The rest is the product's job.
